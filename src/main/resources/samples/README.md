These WAVs were **decoded from the public HQ MP3 previews**, not downloaded as
the original WAV uploads (which require a Freesound login). Decoding to WAV does
not recover detail lost in MP3 compression. They are real recorded hits, not
synthetic imitations. Dundunba and kenkeni are replicas rather than wooden drums.

Downloaded previews:

- https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/170/170180_3015359-hq.mp3
- https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/170/170177_3015359-hq.mp3
- https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/170/170179_3015359-hq.mp3

Processing: decoded to mono 44100 Hz PCM 16-bit little-endian WAV; peak amplitude
normalized to 0.95. No pitch, tempo or tail changes. Leading silence detection
uses a threshold of 1% of source peak with 2 ms pre-roll; all three files required
zero samples of trimming. Approximate durations: 1.899 s / 2.607 s / 2.047 s.
Reproduction script: tools/prepare-drum-samples.py (numpy + soundfile).
